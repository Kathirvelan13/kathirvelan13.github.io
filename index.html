About

A bit about Me

A digital marketer who started to learn marketing with no prior background. I began by learning the fundamentals through an online course, but my real learning started when I began applying those concepts to real campaigns.

Over time, I developed a simple perspective on marketing: Marketing is not just about reaching people — it is about creating measurable business results. I started with Meta Ads, learning how campaigns are structured, how objectives influence performance, and how metrics can be analyzed to improve results. Then, I expanded my knowledge into Google Ads, understanding how businesses can use paid search to reach people who are actively searching for their products or services.

My learning became more practical when I planned and executed a result-focused lead generation campaign for a real client. This experience helped me understand that digital marketing is not just about launching campaigns. It involves understanding the audience, creating the right offer, analyzing performance, and connecting marketing activity to actual business outcomes. I later expanded my focus into SEO, exploring keyword research, on-page optimization, technical SEO and backlinks for building long-term organic visibility.

Currently, I am seeking opportunities in digital marketing where I can apply my skills in SEO, paid advertising, content strategy, and analytics to drive meaningful business growth and measurable results.
